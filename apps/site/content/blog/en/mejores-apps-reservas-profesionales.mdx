Choosing the right booking app can mean the difference between hours of admin work every week or **a schedule that runs itself**. Here's an honest comparison of the top options in 2026 for self-employed professionals.

## How we evaluated the apps

We compared them across 6 practical criteria:

1. **Ease of setup** — How long before your first client can actually book?
2. **AI chatbot** — Does it have a conversational bot, or just a form?
3. **Payments and deposits** — Can it collect a deposit at the time of booking?
4. **Multi-language** — Does it work if your clients are international?
5. **Price** — How much do you pay per month?
6. **Privacy** — Discretion for sensitive services?

## 1. AIBA

**Best for**: professionals who want a **fully automated schedule** with an AI bot on Telegram/WhatsApp.

- ✅ Chatbot powered by Claude Sonnet 4.6 (bookings, cancellations, pricing)
- ✅ Automatic deposits (Stripe/CCBill)
- ✅ 7 languages out-of-the-box
- ✅ Two-way Google Calendar sync
- ✅ Sensitive verticals with enhanced privacy (FaceID, generic titles)
- 💰 From €49/month

## 2. Calendly

**Best for**: B2B consultants, coaches, tech freelancers.

- ✅ Simple, integrates with Zoom and Google Meet
- ❌ No AI chatbot
- ❌ Limited deposit collection (Stripe only, no conditional policies)
- 💰 Free basic plan, Pro from $12/month

## 3. Booksy

**Best for**: hair salons, beauty therapists, barbershops.

- ✅ Very popular in the beauty space, comes with a client marketplace
- ✅ Loyalty features, reviews
- ❌ Requires a public marketplace listing (less discretion)
- 💰 From $30/month per professional

## 4. Fresha

**Best for**: larger salons with multiple professionals.

- ✅ Free for basic use (monetises through payment processing fees)
- ✅ Full management system (inventory, staff)
- ❌ No real AI chatbot
- ❌ Marketplace commission on bookings (~2%)
- 💰 Free + 20% commission on clients acquired via the marketplace

## 5. Timely

**Best for**: mid-range to premium beauty professionals.

- ✅ Polished UX, solid reminder system
- ✅ Accounting integrations
- ❌ Higher price point, no AI bot
- 💰 From $25/month

## 6. Cal.com

**Best for**: developers, tech freelancers, open-source enthusiasts.

- ✅ Open source, self-hosting possible
- ✅ Highly customisable
- ❌ Requires technical knowledge
- ❌ No chatbot or payments out-of-the-box
- 💰 Free (self-hosted) or $15/month cloud

## 7. Google Calendar + WhatsApp Business

**Best for**: professionals just starting out who don't want to spend anything.

- ✅ Free
- ✅ Familiar and easy to use
- ❌ Entirely manual — doesn't scale beyond 15–20 active clients
- 💰 Free

## What to choose depending on your situation

- **Just starting out**: Google Calendar + WhatsApp up to 15–20 active clients.
- **Traditional beauty/aesthetics**: Booksy or Fresha.
- **B2B consultant**: Calendly.
- **Want to automate everything with AI**: AIBA.
- **Sensitive vertical with a need for privacy**: AIBA (the only one with a dual-privacy architecture).
- **Tech-savvy and want full control**: Cal.com self-hosted.

## Conclusion

There's no such thing as a universally "best app." There's only the best app **for your specific situation**. Before you decide, ask yourself:

- How many hours a week do you currently spend managing bookings?
- Are your clients on Telegram/WhatsApp or email?
- Do you need to collect deposits?
- Do you need a special level of discretion?

Answer those 4 questions honestly, and the right choice becomes obvious. And if you do the maths and the difference between free and €50/month gives you back 2 hours a week, the "expensive" app ends up being the cheapest one of all.
